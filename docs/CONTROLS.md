# Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | pause / play |
| `F` | fullscreen |
| Double-click the stage | fullscreen |

Move the mouse to show the hint. After 2.2 s the cursor hides.

## Frame

Internal composition is **960×1200** (4:5). The page scales the whole frame to fit the window, no scroll.

On a **1920×1080** monitor in fullscreen the frame is **864×1080**, centered at x 528–1392.

## Recording

OBS, Display Capture, crop to the frame. One continuous take is enough — the floor does not loop on a seam, it keeps running.
